Domains are customizable aliases for your wallet addresses, simplifying transactions and enhancing usability.

## Features
- **Unique Identifiers**: Each domain is exclusive to the VerifiedX network. VFX domains end in `.vfx` and BTC domains end in `.btc`; the wallet appends the suffix for you.
- **Names**: In the wallet, a domain uses letters and numbers only, up to 65 characters.
- **Cost**: Purchasing a domain burns 5 VFX, plus the transaction fee.
- **Flexibility**: Transfer or delete domains as needed. A transfer or a delete also costs 5 VFX, plus the transaction fee.

## Use Cases
- Simplify wallet interactions by sharing a domain instead of an address.
- Host on-chain websites linked to your domain for decentralized publishing.
- The network supports sending coin and tokens to a VFX domain instead of the address.
